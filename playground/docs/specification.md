Playground is a web-app with objective of providing the employees a way of simulating and visualising the warehouse

## Objectives
- 3D representation of the warehouse layout
- Visual feedback of the hardware (e.g. LEDs)
- Representation of an actor, cart and objects on the warehouse
- Simulate actors interacting within the warehouse (e.g. insertion and removal of objects)
- Provide tools to alter the playground settings (e.g. layout, ?manifesto?, etc)
- Provide a save/load system to restore configurations and states of the playground
- Provide a detailed timeline with all the actions occurred in the playground, and functionalities to roll back to any point in that timeline

## Possible tecnologies
- [Three.js](https://threejs.org/)
- [Grafana](https://grafana.com/)
- [Docker](https://www.docker.com/)
